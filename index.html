Features

Idle at its core. Strategy all the way down.

Galactic Idle blends incremental progression with real 4X decisions: tech trees, fleet composition, faction diplomacy and an economy that pushes back.

⚡

Build & automate

Energy, minerals, credits, research and plasma. Grow a web of structures and balance the upkeep a bigger empire demands.

🧬

Research

A six-branch tech tree with prerequisites. Every breakthrough turns into lasting production.

🚀

Command a fleet

Build ships, deploy task forces and take command of tactical battles — or auto-resolve and let your fleet handle it.

🪐

Conquer the galaxy

Take 20 planets by force or diplomacy, and spend Command Points on doctrines to specialize your empire.

🤝

Five factions

Build reputation through trade and envoys, sign non-aggression pacts and forge alliances that change how you grow.

🧭

Expeditions

Send task forces into 17 dangerous sectors. Weigh risk against reward — a bad outcome can cost ships.
